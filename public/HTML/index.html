Khopra Danda

खोप्रा डाँडा

Alpine meadow with panoramic mountain views above the clouds

Myagdi, Gandaki

Annapurna Municipality, Gandaki Province

About This Place

A stunning high-altitude meadow offering breathtaking 360-degree views of the Annapurna, Dhaulagiri, and Nilgiri ranges. The experience of being above the clouds at sunrise is truly magical.

Trip Information

October - May

3 days / 2 nights

Moderate

Viewpoint

Things to Do

Sunrise hike, sunset watching, local tea houses, photography

Travel Tips

Carry warm clothing, sunscreen, water. No mobile signal above 3000m. Respect the local community.
